Product plan: what each person sees, does, and gets back.

From scope to product: scope said "Linda approves once and the agent pays Marco." Now we know the screens, the exact money wording, how a judge gets in, and what happens when a photo fails, a photo is reused, or the job is over Linda's limit.

The journey, step by step

Screens

Entry page
  • One-line pitch and a story strip: reported → fixed and proven → verified and paid
  • Start the guided demo button
  • Three role cards: Linda, Marco, the tenant. One click, no sign-up
Top bar (always visible)
  • "Viewing as: Linda ▾" switcher that stays on the same job
  • Demo banner with Linda's sandbox PayPal login and copy buttons
  • Reset demo
Tenant
  • Report form: description and one photo
  • Status of their report, ending in "Fixed and verified"
Linda (desktop-friendly)
  • Needs you section, then job tickets
  • Ticket: address, problem, amount, money status, plan, checklist, timeline, photos with stamps
  • Approve and fund with PayPal, or Decline
  • Decision panel: Pay Marco or Cancel job
  • Auto-pay limit, default $100
Marco (mobile-first)
  • Jobs showing the money is held before he starts
  • Big capture button per checklist item, or sample proof photos in demo mode
  • Stamp or "what to fix" per item, with retake
  • Marco's PayPal panel: status, amount, time from proof to paid
Next-step hint (every screen)
  • "Now it's Marco's turn. Continue as Marco →"
  • This is the guided demo, not a separate tour

Behaviors and how we'll know they work

Repair reports
  • The form needs a description and one photo before it sends
  • The tenant sees status through to "Fixed and verified"
Agent job planning
  • Ticket shows a problem summary, cost estimate, and 2–4 photo checklist items
  • Each item describes a photo Marco can actually take
  • Linda can edit the amount and checklist before approving
Approval and funding
  • Approve opens the real PayPal sandbox flow; then "Held on your PayPal, not charged yet"
  • Decline closes the job with nothing held
  • Closing PayPal without approving leaves "Waiting for your approval"
Proof submission
  • Live camera per checklist item
  • Demo sample sets: one passes, one has a problem, one contains a reused photo
  • Can't submit until every item has a photo
AI inspection

Verified  New faucet is installed under the sink with water running.

  • Each item gets a Verified stamp or Problem mark with one plain sentence
  • Failed items say exactly what to fix
  • A reused photo is flagged no matter what the AI thinks of it
  • Text written inside a photo has no effect
Fix and resend
  • Marco resubmits only failed items
  • A second failure sends the job to Linda
Automatic payment
  • All verified, nothing flagged, at or under the limit: pays with no action from Linda
  • Marco sees "Payment on its way", then "Paid $85" with time to paid
  • Linda's ticket shows it was handled without her
  • The limit is enforced by app rules, not the AI
Linda's decisions
  • Over limit, failed twice, or flagged jobs appear under Needs you, with the reason
  • Pay Marco pays him and records her approval
  • Cancel job releases the hold; nobody is charged
  • Changing her limit applies to new decisions
Job record
  • Every ticket shows the full timed history, from report to payment
Demo experience
  • Each visitor gets their own demo data
  • Reset restores the start
  • Seeded jobs: $180 wall patch waiting for Linda, a paid job, a flagged reused-photo job
  • Next-step hint with role switch on every screen

Type, references, and what to avoid
  • Type: warm sans-serif (Figtree or IBM Plex Sans); money in a tabular font, big and plain
  • Feel references only: Mercury, Apple Wallet receipts, Things, a paper job ticket
  • Avoid: purple/blue gradients, AI sparkles, robot icons, chat bubbles, neon or glassy styling, emoji everywhere, vague "AI magic" wording, anything resembling PayPal's own app

Not now

Deferred from the proof of concept
  • Real accounts and logins: demo roles prove the flow
  • SMS or email notifications: in-app status shows the same thing
  • Choosing among handymen: one saved handyman
  • Multiple properties: one is enough to prove it
Later enhancements
  • GPS and time checks, before-and-after matching, random challenge shots
  • 80/20 holdback, trust that builds over time, refunds and disputes, emergency handling
Non-goals
  • Chat interface, marketplace, native apps, real money
  • Claims of being first or fraud-proof
  • Looking like PayPal's own app

Open questions

  • For the spec: protecting the public sandbox login; confirming PayPal hold, capture, and payout steps; choosing the AI provider
