How the product in your PRD gets built: the pieces, how data moves, and where everything lives.

The pieces

The key rule: Claude only gives verdicts. The payment rules, written in code, are the only thing that can start an automatic payment.

One repair, through the system

How the payment rules decide

Needs fix
An item failed on the first try. Marco retakes only that item.
Needs Linda
Failed again, a reused photo, Claude unavailable, or over her limit.
Pay
Everything passed and the job is at or under the limit (default $100).

The parts, and what they serve

Demo sessions and seeding · Demo experience

A private demo ID per visitor, kept in a cookie. Seeds: the $180 wall patch (live from approval onward), plus two labeled sample jobs. Reset wipes and reseeds. lib/demo.ts

Screens · Screens and Layout

Entry page, /demo/linda|marco|tenant shortcuts, tenant view, Linda's list and ticket, Marco's list and job screen. Top bar and next-step hint on every screen. app/, components/

Repair reports and job planner · Repair reports, Agent job planning

Text plus a photo go to Claude, which returns a summary, estimate, 2–4 checklist items, and any uncertainty notes. app/api/reports, lib/ai.ts

PayPal client · Approval and funding, Automatic payment
  • Through PayPal's Server SDK where it has the call; direct calls otherwise.
  • Create order (authorize) → POST /v2/checkout/orders
  • Authorize → POST /v2/checkout/orders/{id}/authorize
  • Take the money → POST /v2/payments/authorizations/{id}/capture
  • Release the hold → POST /v2/payments/authorizations/{id}/void
  • Pay Marco → POST /v1/payments/payouts
  • Check payment → GET /v1/payments/payouts/{batchId}

lib/paypal.ts

Photo checks and inspector · Proof submission, AI inspection

Photos are shrunk and fingerprinted, and near-matches with earlier photos are flagged as reused. Claude then returns a verdict and reason per item. Text inside photos is treated as evidence, never instructions. lib/photos.ts, lib/ai.ts

Payment rules · Automatic payment, Fix and resend

Pure code. The only place an automatic payment can start. lib/rules.ts

Linda's decisions, limit, and payment status · Linda's decisions

Pay Marco or Cancel (void). Limit changes apply from then on. Marco's panel asks for payment status every few seconds while it's on its way.

Job record and spending guard · Job record

Every status change writes a timed event, and the ticket timeline is built from these. A cap of 20 Claude calls per visitor, plus a spend limit in the Anthropic Console.

If something breaks

Payouts don't work in the sandbox

Tested first, on day one. Try enabling Payouts, then the fallback of setting Marco as the payee. If neither works, say plainly what is simulated.

Claude fails or is slow

"Couldn't check this right now." Never auto-pays; the job goes to Linda.

The demo PayPal login stops working

Swap to the backup Linda account in Render's settings. Check the login daily during judging.

The camera is blocked

Sample photo sets and a normal file picker.
